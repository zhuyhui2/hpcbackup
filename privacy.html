Privacy Policy for HPCbackup

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Purpose

HPCbackup is a personal research data backup tool using rclone and Google Drive.

Google Drive Access

HPCbackup uses the Google Drive API to access, upload, download, and manage files as authorized by the account owner.

Data Privacy

Research files are transferred between the owner's HPC system and Google Drive. Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising.

Data Retention and Security

Files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the account owner. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the rclone configuration. Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
